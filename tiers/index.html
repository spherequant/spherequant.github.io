Tiers

Participation Tiers

The program is offered in three participation tiers to accommodate different levels of involvement, feedback, and mentorship.

All tiers provide access to the full technical curriculum and core research infrastructure.

Core — ₹40,000 + GST

Designed for independent practitioners who want full access to the curriculum and cohort environment.

Includes:

  • Full access to all technical modules
  • Weekly live walkthrough sessions
  • Group research and system reviews
  • Access to shared cohort repositories and scaffolding
  • One year access to the SphereQuant Slack community
  • Ongoing cohort discussions and peer collaboration

Best suited for:

  • Experienced traders comfortable working independently
  • Participants primarily seeking systems and frameworks
  • Those who prefer peer-driven iteration

Pro — ₹75,000 + GST

Designed for practitioners who want structured feedback and closer technical supervision.

Includes everything in Core, plus:

  • Weekly small-group code and system reviews
  • Two 30-minute one-on-one review sessions during the program
  • Personalized feedback on research design and backtests
  • Priority access to office hours
  • Direct Slack access to program faculty

Best suited for:

  • Portfolio managers and serious traders
  • Participants building production workflows
  • Those who want early correction and architectural guidance

Mentor — ₹1,25,000 + GST

Designed for senior practitioners and capital allocators seeking personalized system design and research supervision.

Includes everything in Pro, plus:

  • Weekly one-on-one mentorship sessions
  • Personalized research and system architecture audit
  • Review of participant’s existing trading and research stack
  • Priority scheduling for office hours and reviews
  • Extended Slack access for one year post-program
  • Optional institutional-grade market data access (where applicable)

Best suited for:

  • Portfolio managers
  • Proprietary traders
  • Professionals managing significant capital
  • Participants seeking long-term mentorship and system design guidance

Data Access (Optional)

Participants may opt into institutional-grade market data packages where available.

This includes:

  • NSE equities and derivatives
  • Historical intraday datasets
  • Corporate actions and adjusted histories

Pricing and availability depend on cohort and provider terms and will be communicated separately.

Cohort Limits

To maintain depth of review and mentorship:

  • Core tier is limited to approximately 30 participants
  • Pro tier is limited to approximately 10 participants
  • Mentor tier is limited to approximately 3–5 participants

Cohorts do not overlap.

Selection Process

Participation is selective.

Admission is based on:

  • Market experience
  • Capital responsibility
  • Technical readiness
  • Alignment with program philosophy

This is intentional.

The program is designed for practitioners with real capital, real responsibility, and long-term intent.
