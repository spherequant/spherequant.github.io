The problem

Bad research is now the cheapest it has ever been.

An AI can write a backtest in thirty seconds. It will happily use tomorrow's prices, forget delisted stocks, ignore STT and slippage, and hand you a beautiful equity curve. Without a process, AI just makes you confidently wrong, faster.

What it can't do is ask the questions only a trader knows to ask. That's your edge. QUARK makes those questions testable — on clean data, with an analyst that checks its own work.

How the data is organised

Raw → derived → factors.

The same structure professional desks use. Each layer is built only from the one below it, so any result can be rebuilt from source — and nothing from the future leaks into a test.

01 · Raw

Exactly as received

Exchange files, corporate actions, contract specs, trading calendar. Never edited.

02 · Derived

Clean and adjusted

Adjusted prices, continuous futures, option chains, index members over time.

03 · Factors

Ready to research

Momentum, volatility, liquidity, basis, OI change, IV, event flags — one row per instrument per day.

Plus the intraday store: 200 GB of hemis_research data for intraday studies. Useful intraday measures can be promoted into daily factors.
