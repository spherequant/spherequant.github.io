SphereQuant offers advanced training programs focused on building institutional-grade quantitative research and portfolio systems.

Our training is designed for experienced market practitioners who want to professionalize their research workflows, engineering discipline, and decision-making infrastructure using modern quantitative methods and AI-augmented development.

This is not trading education.  
This is not a signal program.

**This is research systems training.**

---

## Flagship Program
### AI-Augmented Quantitative Research & Portfolio Systems

An advanced cohort-based program for experienced traders and investors to design, validate, and operate end-to-end quantitative research and portfolio infrastructure.

Participants build a complete personal research environment covering:

- Market data pipelines and adjustment frameworks
- Event-driven and strategy backtesting systems
- Statistical hypothesis testing tools
- Multi-asset and portfolio construction logic
- Risk analytics and rebalancing systems
- Research and monitoring dashboards
- AI-assisted research and engineering workflows

The program emphasizes:

- Correctness over speed
- Robustness over optimization
- Reproducibility over performance claims
- Process over predictions

No signals.  
No forecasts.  
No performance promises.

---

## Program Objective

The objective of this program is to enable participants to:

- Convert discretionary hypotheses into testable research questions
- Build reproducible and auditable research pipelines
- Design backtests that survive scrutiny and regime changes
- Construct portfolios with disciplined risk and rebalancing logic
- Use AI as a supervised junior quant and research engineer
- Operate a complete personal research environment for long-term use

Participants leave with a **production-quality research and portfolio system** that becomes part of their permanent trading workflow.

---

## Who This Program Is For

This program is designed for:

- Experienced discretionary traders
- Systematic traders without deep engineering backgrounds
- Portfolio managers and serious investors
- Proprietary traders building independent research stacks

Typical participants:

- Trade or invest with meaningful capital
- Have several years of market experience
- Understand drawdowns and regime risk
- Want to professionalize research and validation

This program is **not suitable** for:

- Beginners in markets
- Low-capital traders
- Those seeking signals or shortcuts
- Those expecting profitability guarantees

## Teaching Philosophy

We teach only what we practice.

Principles that guide the program:

- No performance promises
- No signal selling
- No black-box models
- No data mining from randomness

Core focus areas:

- Converting discretionary intuition into research
- Designing clean and auditable datasets
- Building backtests that detect false edges
- Understanding failure modes and research bias
- Thinking at the portfolio and risk level

AI is used throughout as a **research and engineering amplifier** — treated as a supervised junior quant and developer, not as a signal generator.

Participants learn how to:

- Plan research using structured prompting
- Supervise and audit AI-generated code
- Detect silent failures and hallucinated logic
- Accelerate iteration without sacrificing rigor

## Program Structure

The program runs as a structured cohort over approximately **8 weeks**, with an optional advanced capstone phase.

Each week builds a concrete system component that remains part of the final research stack.

High-level arc:

- Research environment and AI workflows
- Market data pipelines and adjustment frameworks
- Backtesting and validation systems
- Statistical research and event studies
- Multi-asset and portfolio systems
- Monitoring and dashboard infrastructure
- Capstone research and system integration

The program combines:

- Pre-recorded technical modules
- Weekly live walkthroughs
- System and code reviews
- Office hours and mentorship
- Capstone project supervision

## Time Commitment

- Weekend participation for live sessions
- Independent implementation between sessions

This program is designed for practitioners who treat trading and research as a serious professional activity.
