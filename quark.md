<section class="section">
  <p class="section-label">The problem</p>
  <h2>Bad research is now the cheapest it has ever been.</h2>
  <p>An AI can write a backtest in thirty seconds. It will happily use tomorrow's prices, forget delisted stocks, ignore STT and slippage, and hand you a beautiful equity curve. Without a process, AI just makes you confidently wrong, faster.</p>
  <p class="lead-follow">What it can't do is ask the questions only a trader knows to ask. That's your edge. QUARK makes those questions testable — on clean data, with an analyst that checks its own work.</p>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <p class="section-label">How the data is organised</p>
  <h2>Raw → derived → factors.</h2>
  <p>The same structure professional desks use. Each layer is built only from the one below it, so any result can be rebuilt from source — and nothing from the future leaks into a test.</p>

  <div class="layers">
    <div class="layer">
      <span class="layer-tag">01 · Raw</span>
      <h3>Exactly as received</h3>
      <p>Exchange files, corporate actions, contract specs, trading calendar. Never edited.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="layer">
      <span class="layer-tag">02 · Derived</span>
      <h3>Clean and adjusted</h3>
      <p>Adjusted prices, continuous futures, option chains, index members over time.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="layer">
      <span class="layer-tag">03 · Factors</span>
      <h3>Ready to research</h3>
      <p>Momentum, volatility, liquidity, basis, OI change, IV, event flags — one row per instrument per day.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <p class="layers-note">Plus the intraday store: 200 GB of hemis_research data for intraday studies. Useful intraday measures can be promoted into daily factors.</p>
</section>
