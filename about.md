SphereQuant is the quantitative research and trading division of Hemisphere Research Capital.

We focus on building institutional-grade research infrastructure and systematic trading systems for futures, commodities, and energy markets. Our work sits at the intersection of market microstructure, data engineering, and disciplined portfolio construction.
<br>

---

## Our Philosophy

We believe durable trading edges rarely come from models alone.

They come from:

- Clean and correctly adjusted data  
- Robust research workflows  
- Auditable backtesting and validation  
- A deep understanding of market structure and regime behavior  

Infrastructure is the primary edge.

Our goal is to create systems that remain reliable under stress, scale with complexity, and remain interpretable to the portfolio manager using them.
<br>

---


## Research Approach

Our research process is designed to be reproducible, skeptical, and regime-aware.

Key principles guide our work:

- Every hypothesis must be testable  
- Every backtest must be auditable  
- Every result must survive alternative specifications  
- No result is trusted without understanding how it can fail  
<br>
We emphasize:

- Event-driven research  
- Term structure and curve dynamics  
- Cross-asset and cross-contract behavior  
- Portfolio-level risk and drawdown control  

We explicitly study how backtests lie, how edges decay, and how research pipelines introduce hidden bias.
<br>
