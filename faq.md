    <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary>Is this a trading course?</summary>
      <div class="faq-answer">
        <p>No.</p>
        <p>This program does not teach trading strategies, signals, or entry/exit systems.</p>
        <p>It teaches how to design, validate, and operate quantitative research and portfolio infrastructure.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary>Will this make me profitable?</summary>
      <div class="faq-answer">
        <p>No guarantees are offered.</p>
        <p>Profitability depends on execution, discipline, risk management, and market conditions — none of which can be taught as formulas.</p>
        <p>We teach process, infrastructure, and validation.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary>Do you teach machine learning or AI trading models?</summary>
      <div class="faq-answer">
        <p>No.</p>
        <p>We do not teach predictive modeling, neural networks, or data mining.</p>
        <p>AI is used strictly as a research and engineering amplifier — not as a signal generator.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary>What market knowledge is required?</summary>
      <div class="faq-answer">
        <p>This program is not suitable for beginners. Participants should already:</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Understand market structure and instruments</li>
          <li>Have several years of trading or investing experience</li>
          <li>Be familiar with drawdowns and regime risk</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary>How technical do I need to be?</summary>
      <div class="faq-answer">
        <p>No prior professional programming experience is required. However, participants must be comfortable:</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Working with Python</li>
          <li>Reading and modifying code</li>
          <li>Debugging with assistance</li>
        </ul>
        <p>We explicitly teach how to supervise AI for coding and debugging.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary>Do you provide trading capital or job placement?</summary>
      <div class="faq-answer">
        <p>No.</p>
        <p>This program does not provide capital, internships, job placement, or recruitment.</p>
        <p>It is designed for independent practitioners and professionals managing their own or institutional capital.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary>What assets does the program cover?</summary>
      <div class="faq-answer">
        <p>The program is asset-agnostic.</p>
        <p>Initial implementations use:</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Equities</li>
          <li>Futures</li>
          <li>Indices</li>
        </ul>
        <p>Frameworks generalize to:</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Commodities</li>
          <li>Rates</li>
          <li>FX</li>
          <li>Crypto (where data quality permits)</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary>How much time is required?</summary>
      <div class="faq-answer">
        <p>Typical commitment:</p>
        <ul>
          <li>6–10 hours per week</li>
          <li>Weekend participation for live sessions</li>
          <li>Independent implementation between sessions</li>
        </ul>
        <p>Participants treating this as a hobby will struggle.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary>Can I use my own data?</summary>
      <div class="faq-answer">
        <p>Yes.</p>
        <p>Participants are encouraged to use their own datasets where possible.</p>
        <p>We also provide reference pipelines and optional institutional data access.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary>Will you debug my code?</summary>
      <div class="faq-answer">
        <p>No.</p>
        <p>We do not provide debugging services.</p>
        <p>We teach how to:</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Design debuggable systems</li>
          <li>Use AI to debug correctly</li>
          <li>Detect silent failures and incorrect logic</li>
        </ul>
        <p>Code reviews focus on architecture and research logic.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary>Are sessions recorded?</summary>
      <div class="faq-answer">
        <p>Yes.</p>
        <p>All technical modules and live walkthroughs are recorded for cohort access.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary>What happens after the program?</summary>
      <div class="faq-answer">
        <p>Participants retain:</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Their complete research system</li>
          <li>Repository scaffolding</li>
          <li>Dashboards and pipelines</li>
          <li>Slack community access (one year)</li>
        </ul>
        <p>Advanced cohorts and collaborations may be offered selectively.</p>
      </div>
    </details>
