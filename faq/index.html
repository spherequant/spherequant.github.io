FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a trading course?

No.

This program does not teach trading strategies, signals, or entry/exit systems.

It teaches how to design, validate, and operate quantitative research and portfolio infrastructure.

Will this make me profitable?

No guarantees are offered.

Profitability depends on execution, discipline, risk management, and market conditions — none of which can be taught as formulas.

We teach process, infrastructure, and validation.

Do you teach machine learning or AI trading models?

No.

We do not teach predictive modeling, neural networks, or data mining.

AI is used strictly as a research and engineering amplifier — not as a signal generator.

What market knowledge is required?

Participants should already:

  • Understand market structure and instruments
  • Have several years of trading or investing experience
  • Be familiar with drawdowns and regime risk

This program is not suitable for beginners.

How technical do I need to be?

No prior professional programming experience is required.

However, participants must be comfortable:

  • Working with Python
  • Reading and modifying code
  • Debugging with assistance

We explicitly teach how to supervise AI for coding and debugging.

Do you provide trading capital or job placement?

No.

This program does not provide capital, internships, job placement, or recruitment.

It is designed for independent practitioners and professionals managing their own or institutional capital.

What assets does the program cover?

The program is asset-agnostic.

Initial implementations use:

  • Equities
  • Futures
  • Indices

Frameworks generalize to:

  • Commodities
  • Rates
  • FX
  • Crypto (where data quality permits)

How much time is required?

Typical commitment:

  • 6–10 hours per week
  • Weekend participation for live sessions
  • Independent implementation between sessions

Participants treating this as a hobby will struggle.

Can I use my own data?

Yes.

Participants are encouraged to use their own datasets where possible.

We also provide reference pipelines and optional institutional data access.

Will you debug my code?

No.

We do not provide debugging services.

We teach how to:

  • Design debuggable systems
  • Use AI to debug correctly
  • Detect silent failures and incorrect logic

Code reviews focus on architecture and research logic.

Are sessions recorded?

Yes.

All technical modules and live walkthroughs are recorded for cohort access.

What happens after the program?

Participants retain:

  • Their complete research system
  • Repository scaffolding
  • Dashboards and pipelines
  • Slack community access (one year)

Advanced cohorts and collaborations may be offered selectively.
