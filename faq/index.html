FAQ

Before you apply

What is QUARK, in one line?

Your own market research database, 200 GB of intraday data and an AI analyst that knows it — set up in week one, so you can spend the rest of the cohort testing your own trading ideas.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. On the Practitioner track, setup is guided step by step and your AI analyst writes the code. You learn to ask good questions and check its work.

If you can already code, the Builder track lets you extend the desk with your own factors, datasets and dashboards.

What market experience do I need?

You should already trade or invest actively, understand the instruments you use, and have lived through drawdowns. QUARK is not for beginners in markets.

Is this a trading course? Will it make me profitable?

No, and no guarantees. QUARK doesn't give you signals, tips or strategies to copy.

It gives you the data, tools and process to test your own ideas properly — so you stop trading the ones that don't hold up.

The AI analyst

Which AI do I need, and what does it cost?

QUARK runs on OpenCode with DeepSeek 4.1 Flash, which is free to use there. You don't need any AI subscription.

If you prefer Claude Code, the same kit works there too.

Does the AI pick trades for me?

No. The AI is your research analyst, not a signal generator. It writes queries, runs tests and builds dashboards — and the /bias-audit skill checks its results for common mistakes before you see them.

Can the AI break my data?

No. The analyst gets read-only access to the database. The update scripts run separately, and no broker keys are ever involved.

Data and setup

What data is included?

Two parts:

  • Daily research database for stocks, futures, options and indices, organised as raw → derived → factors and kept current by the update scripts.
  • hemis_research intraday data, about 200 GB, for intraday studies.
How do I get the data?

Through Google Drive, split into packs by instrument and year. Packs are released from 20 November so you can download before the cohort starts. We recommend rclone, which resumes interrupted downloads and verifies files.

What computer do I need?

A reasonably modern laptop or desktop (Windows, Mac or Linux) with at least 500 GB of free SSD space for the intraday data.

Short on space? Start with a smaller intraday set, such as index futures and options only, and add more later. The daily database itself is small.

Can I use my own data?

Yes. The QUARK schema has a place for new sources, and the Builder track covers adding them.

The cohort

When is it, and how much time does it take?

Cohort 2 runs from 1 December 2026 to 15 January 2027, with pre-work from 20 November. Expect about 6–8 hours a week, including a live session each Saturday. The Christmas–New Year period is a light, self-paced capstone window.

Are sessions recorded?

Yes. Every lesson and live session is recorded.

Will you fix my code?

We support everything we ship: the schema, scripts, dashboards and skills. If something you add yourself breaks, we review it with you and show you how to get your analyst to fix it.

What happens after the cohort?

Your desk keeps running and stays yours. For 12 months you also get monthly live research clinics, new factors and skills, and community access.

I was in batch 1. What changes for me?

Your access continues until your one-year period ends. Get in touch about moving your own build onto the QUARK schema.
