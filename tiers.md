## Data Access (Optional)

Participants may opt into institutional-grade market data packages where available.

This includes:

- NSE equities and derivatives  
- Historical intraday datasets  
- Corporate actions and adjusted histories  

Pricing and availability depend on cohort and provider terms and will be communicated separately.

---

## Cohort Limits

To maintain depth of review and mentorship:

- Core tier is limited to approximately 30 participants  
- Pro tier is limited to approximately 10 participants  
- Mentor tier is limited to approximately 3–5 participants  

Cohorts do not overlap.

---

## Selection Process

Participation is selective.

Admission is based on:

- Market experience  
- Capital responsibility  
- Technical readiness  
- Alignment with program philosophy  

This is intentional.

The program is designed for practitioners with real capital, real responsibility, and long-term intent.
