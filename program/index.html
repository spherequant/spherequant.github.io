Program

AI-Augmented Quantitative Research & Portfolio Systems

An advanced cohort program for experienced market practitioners to design, validate, and operate institutional-grade quantitative research and portfolio systems.

This program trains traders and investors to build complete research infrastructure — from raw data ingestion to portfolio-level analytics — using modern engineering discipline and AI-augmented workflows.

This is not a trading course.
This is not a signal program.
This is training in research systems and decision infrastructure.

Program Objective

The objective of this program is to enable participants to:

  • Convert discretionary market hypotheses into testable research questions
  • Build reproducible and auditable research pipelines
  • Design backtests that withstand scrutiny and regime changes
  • Construct portfolios with disciplined risk and rebalancing logic
  • Use AI as a supervised junior quant and research engineer
  • Operate a complete personal research environment for long-term use

Participants leave with a production-quality research and portfolio system that becomes part of their permanent trading workflow.

Who This Program Is For

This program is designed for:

  • Experienced discretionary traders
  • Systematic traders without engineering depth
  • Portfolio managers and serious investors
  • Proprietary traders building independent research stacks

Typical participants:

  • Have several years of market experience
  • Trade or invest with meaningful personal or professional capital
  • Understand drawdowns, volatility, and regime risk
  • Want to professionalize their research and validation process

This program is not suitable for:

  • Beginners in markets
  • Low-capital traders
  • Those seeking trading signals or shortcuts
  • Those expecting profitability guarantees

What You Will Build

Every participant builds a complete end-to-end research and portfolio system.

Research Infrastructure

A structured Python repository with version control, clean environments, and a modular framework for strategy development.

Data & Adjustment Pipelines

Pipelines for market data ingestion, corporate action adjustments, futures contract rolling, and survivorship-aware datasets.

Backtesting & Strategy Systems

A robust engine for single-instrument and universe backtests, parameter sweeps, and performance diagnostics.

Statistical Research Tools

Frameworks for event studies, conditional return analysis, distribution studies, and edge decay testing.

Portfolio Systems

Logic for portfolio construction, rebalancing, risk analytics, and performance monitoring at the portfolio level.

Research & Monitoring Dashboards

Grafana-based dashboards for research, strategy monitoring, and custom visual analytics in your daily workflow.

Teaching Philosophy

We teach only what we practice.

Guiding principles:

  • No performance promises
  • No signal selling
  • No black-box models
  • No data mining from randomness

We emphasize:

  • Correctness before complexity
  • Process before outcomes
  • Robustness before optimization
  • Understanding failure before chasing alpha

A core component of the program is learning how backtests lie, how edges decay, and how research pipelines introduce hidden bias.

AI is used throughout the program as a research and engineering amplifier — treated as a supervised junior quant and developer, not as a signal generator.

Participants learn how to:

  • Plan research using structured prompting
  • Supervise and audit AI-generated code
  • Detect silent failures and hallucinated logic
  • Accelerate iteration without sacrificing rigor

Program Structure

The program runs as a structured cohort over approximately 8 weeks, with an optional advanced capstone phase.

Each week focuses on building a concrete system component that remains part of the participant’s final research stack.

High-level arc:

  • Research environment and AI workflows
  • Market data pipelines and adjustment frameworks
  • Backtesting and validation systems
  • Statistical research and event studies
  • Multi-asset and portfolio systems
  • Monitoring and dashboard infrastructure
  • Capstone research and system integration

The program combines:

  • Pre-recorded technical modules
  • Weekly live walkthroughs
  • System and code reviews
  • Office hours and mentorship
  • Capstone project supervision

Time Commitment

Expected commitment:

  • 6–10 hours per week
  • Weekend participation for live sessions and reviews
  • Independent implementation between sessions

This program is designed for practitioners who treat trading and research as a serious professional activity.

Tools & Stack

Participants work with a production-oriented stack including:

  • Python (research and strategy development)
  • Git and GitHub (version control and workflow)
  • PostgreSQL (market and research data)
  • Grafana (research and portfolio dashboards)
  • Backtesting and analytics libraries
  • AI assistants integrated into the development environment

The program is asset-agnostic, with initial implementations using liquid and accessible datasets.

Admission & Cohort Size

Cohort sizes are intentionally limited to maintain depth of review and mentorship.

Admission is based on:

  • Market experience
  • Capital responsibility
  • Technical readiness
  • Alignment with program philosophy

We explicitly avoid admitting participants for whom this program would not be appropriate.

Outcomes

By the end of the program, participants will:

  • Operate a complete personal research environment
  • Run auditable backtests and hypothesis tests
  • Maintain clean, adjusted, and scalable datasets
  • Construct and analyze portfolios systematically
  • Monitor strategies and portfolios via dashboards
  • Use AI effectively as part of their research workflow

More importantly, participants develop:

  • Research discipline
  • Statistical skepticism
  • Engineering rigor
  • Portfolio-level thinking

These skills compound over years.
