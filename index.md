# Quantitative Research, Trading, and Portfolio Management

Our work combines rigorous research, data engineering, and a deep understanding of market structure to navigate the complexities of systematic trading.

We believe infrastructure is the primary edge. Our focus is on creating robust, auditable, and reproducible systems that perform across market regimes and support disciplined capital allocation.

Our core domains are equities and futures markets (primarily commodities and energy), with an emphasis on term structure, microstructure, and quantitative factor modeling.

SphereQuant operates as the quantitative research and trading division of Hemisphere Research Capital.

We partner with a select group of asset managers, proprietary trading desks, and research institutions. For collaboration inquiries, please contact us at contact@spherequant.com.
